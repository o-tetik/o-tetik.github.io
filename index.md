## Ödül Tetik

oeduel.tetik [sign-of-the-devil] univie.ac.at

In 2024 I obtained my Dr. sc. nat. under [Alberto S. Cattaneo](https://www.math.uzh.ch/index.php?id=people&key1=116) in Zurich. Before that, I did my MSc in Bonn (under Mark Penney and Peter Teichner), and my BSc in Göttingen (under Thomas Schick). I vaguely remember graduating from high school in Istanbul.

Between 2024-2028 I'm a postdoc with [Nils Carqueville](https://www.carqueville.net/nils/index.php) in Vienna. I'm also a long-term guest in the Cattaneo group in Zurich.

My wife [Tabea Tetik-Moschettini](https://kzu.ch/personen/lehrpersonen/mathematik) is a teacher at the KZU and KUE in Zurich. Our two cats Konrad and Cleofas are also busy.
